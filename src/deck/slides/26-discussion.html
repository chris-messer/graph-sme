Map your needs to a semantic layer and a starting rung

The proposal

Write down the questions that must work. Decide for each whether it is a taxonomy, ontology, or knowledge-graph question. Then start at the lowest rung that can answer it, and climb only when it demonstrably falls short.

Which questions must work on day one — rollups, multi-hop traversal, algorithms, or formal reasoning?
How are your hierarchies stored today, and which semantic rules do not fit that shape?
Has the managed semantic stack been run against those questions, and what happened?
Are you asking about the meaning of your assets, or about how individual records connect?
Who owns semantic curation, and what latency and concurrency does production need?
