Four open source Labs projects, each owning a different tier of meaning

All four deploy into your own workspace and read the same Unity Catalog assets. None of them is a rival to the others — the question is which tier your programme is actually short of.

Tier and project Fits when Fails when The tradeoff
Tier 1 · Describe what you already have dbxmetagen GenAI-assisted metadata toolkit with an ontology layer. Classifies tables and columns into a configurable entity taxonomy and emits UC tags, metric views or a knowledge graph. Not sized in source
Nobody has written down what the assets mean yet, and hand-curation would stall the programme before it starts. You need formal semantics. A taxonomy of entity types is not an ontology and will not infer anything. AI-proposed classifications need a human to validate them before anything downstream is allowed to trust them.
Tier 2 · Answer in place, store nothing Ontop VKG Virtual Knowledge Graph forked to run as a Databricks App. SPARQL translates at runtime into SQL over UC tables through R2RML mappings, with JDBC federation to sources outside the lakehouse. Not sized in source
The data cannot be copied, or it genuinely lives across several systems and you need one SPARQL surface over all of it. The questions are traversal-heavy. Every hop becomes runtime SQL and the whole cost lands on whoever asked. Nothing materialized means nothing to refresh — and nothing precomputed to lean on when a query gets expensive.
Tier 3 · Materialize and reason OntoBricks OWL ontology design, R2RML mapping to UC tables, triples materialized to a Delta triple store plus Lakebase, OWL 2 RL / SWRL / SHACL reasoning, generated GraphQL API, ontology tools exposed over MCP. Low–Medium · days to a working graph, 1–2 weeks to a curated ontology
The client wants formal semantics — inferred classes, rules, validation — or you want to pressure-test in days whether they need one at all. The real problem is organizational rather than logical. No amount of OWL settles a vocabulary nobody owns. A materialized triple store is another copy of the truth, on a refresh schedule someone has to own.
Tier 4 · Govern what the meaning is Ontos Data products and contracts on ODCS/ODPS, a semantic-model layer binding UC assets to business concepts, domains and stewardship, a declarative compliance DSL, review workflows with audit trails, MCP. Medium · 2–4 weeks for the first domains
The vocabulary itself is contested, and the programme needs named owners, contracts and review to survive its own pilot. You need inference. Ontos governs what a term means; it does not derive new facts from it. Organizational adoption is the work here, not the software. The app installs in an afternoon; the agreement does not.

Reading this slideThe source treats tiers 3 and 4 as primary and pairs them rather than choosing between them — both sit alongside a Genie stack over the same assets. Tiers 1 and 2 carry no effort figure in it, so we would size those with you.
