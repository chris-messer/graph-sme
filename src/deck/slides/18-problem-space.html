18 Problem space

Two independent decisions sit inside every graph retrieval, and they fail in different ways

One picture, two controls. A user asks for “everything connected to Delta Holdings” — three entities match that string, and the answer is however far out you choose to walk. Move either control and the frontier changes for a completely different reason.

Problem 1 Which node do we start from?
Problem 2 How far out do we walk?
Get problem 1 wrong and the answer is wrong at every depth Resolve to the wrong company and every hop after it is confidently, silently wrong. No amount of tuning the traversal recovers from it, and nothing in the output looks broken.
Get problem 2 wrong and the answer is right but unaffordable Walk to three hops here and a shared registered-agent address enters the frontier, dragging everything attached to it in. The answer is still correct. The bill and the latency are not.

Why this slide existsAlmost every “we need a graph database” conversation is really one of these two problems, and the two have completely different option sets. The next two slides take them one at a time, in this order.
